MATERIAL WEIGHT MODEL INTEGRATION

Workflow
- MAIN_AIRCRAFT_DESIGN_CODE.m selects WeightModelChoice (Component or Raymer),
  config_row = 1, component_row = 7, and initial W0_guess = 55 lb.
- Component_Data -> Read_Material_Weight.m -> Weight_Data / CG_Data ->
  Design_Input.Material_Empty_lb -> sizing.m. Payload comes from W_pay.
- Component mode holds empty weight fixed while sizing gross/battery weight:
  W0_calc = (We + W_crew + W_pay_fixed)/(1-BMF).
- Raymer mode recalculates statistical empty weight each iteration. It remains
  the fallback when Material_Empty_lb is absent; the models are never blended.
- Figure 710 compares Raymer categories once at the component-sized final W0.
  Changing component weight changes W0 and therefore the Raymer estimates.

Spreadsheet rules
- Shell weight = density * wetted area * thickness * 1.05.
  Units: lb/ft^3, ft^2, ft, and lb; CG/leading-edge positions are in ft.
- Positive structural weight overrides the estimate (no 1.05 multiplier).
  Zero/blank structural weight requests a shell estimate.
- Each blank/invalid/nonpositive thickness defaults independently to
  2.5 mm = 0.0082021 ft. Valid spreadsheet thickness always overrides it.
- Zero/blank nose, payload, ballast or systems weight adds nothing;
  these items are NOT automatically estimated. Supply materials, densities
  and geometry for shell estimates; zero density/area is not an unknown value.
- Structural Xcg = 0 requests a geometry estimate; do not leave it blank.
  Supply wing/tail X_LE. For nose/payload/ballast/systems, Xcg = 0 means the datum.
- Empty weight excludes payload and the mission-sized battery.
  Weight_Data.Wo = empty + payload; it is not the sized gross weight.

Systems and landing gear (Component_Data, Excel row 8)
- W_systems (T8) = 7.3 lb: 5.3 lb systems + 2.0 lb fixed landing gear.
  Both appear under Systems; do not add landing gear again separately.
- Systems budget: motors 870 g; ESCs 250 g; six servos 360 g;
  flight controller/GPS 66.6 g; radio/power/wiring 430 g;
  props/mounts/linkages 400 g. Total 2376.6 g, rounded to 5.3 lb.
- Gear budget: legs/strut 530 g; wheels 164 g; steering servo 60 g;
  mounting hardware 150 g. Total 904 g, rounded to 2.0 lb.
- These are reference-hardware weights plus allowances, not selected hardware
  or a landing-load design. Unmodeled spars/ribs/bulkheads remain excluded.
- Xcg_systems (U8) = 1.9735 ft: temporary fuselage midpoint; replace when known.
